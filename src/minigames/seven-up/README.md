# 7Up

Motivator: Chance. Author: [SwagatSBhuyan].

## Mechanic

Start with $100. Bet a whole-dollar amount on a sum under 7, exactly 7, or over 7. A correct under/over prediction returns 2× the bet; exactly 7 returns 5×, including the stake. A wrong prediction loses the bet. Reach $1,000 to win; lose your bankroll and the game ends. Seven Boost changes the dice: off, both are fair; on, a sum of 7 occurs on one third of rolls, twice its usual probability.

## Dynamic

Each roll resolves a visible risk. Two anonymous playtesters said Seven Boost made the goal easier when betting exactly 7; one kept choosing that bet. The other found the boost less helpful for under/over bets. With it off, they found the randomized rolls more challenging, thought more about predictions, and varied bet sizes. They said exact-7 bets in this mode led to bankruptcy more often than not. The changed odds affected which bets seemed worthwhile and how carefully one player wagered.

## Aesthetic

One playtester called Seven Boost on “a sandbox” for experimenting. With it off, the game felt more strategic: money management mattered, and reaching the goal felt more rewarding. They recommended off for new players, while saying on can be a fun start depending on a player's gaming experience. The odds made the game feel playful or strategic.

## Sources

The Chance section says, “chance means uncertainty, and uncertainty means surprises” (Schell 2019, p. 193). Changing the probability of seven shows how a rule shapes uncertainty. I kept Codex's suggestion to change that probability with one toggle. No outside code, art, or sound was borrowed.
