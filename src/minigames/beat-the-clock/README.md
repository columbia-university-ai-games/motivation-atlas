# Beat the Clock

Motivator: Challenge and mastery (`challenge`). Author: lzy2318831.

## Mechanic

Play eight one-shot pool layouts. Choose Easy (1 point) or Hard (3 points).
Only the chosen ball scores; scratches score zero. Gray obstacles award
nothing. Maximum: 24.

Press the stick or white-ball ring, pull back, and release. More pull adds
power; sideways movement adjusts aim. Taps do not fire; Escape cancels.
Keyboard aiming, Power and Shoot remain available.

Time Pressure is the only toggle: ON gives 60 seconds; OFF removes the
deadline. Other rules stay identical. New round resets setup.

## Dynamic

**Observed behavior:** One classmate spent more time carefully aiming with
Time Pressure OFF. With it ON, the player made shooting decisions faster.

**Interpretation:** The deadline appeared to encourage quicker decisions
for this player. One playtest cannot establish how other players would respond.

## Aesthetic

**Reported feeling:** The player felt more nervous with the countdown enabled.

**Interpretation:** This suggests time pressure added tension for this player.
No other observations, scores, completion times, or feelings were recorded.
