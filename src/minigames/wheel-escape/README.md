# Wheel Escape

Motivator: Challenge and mastery. Author: rogerwangdev.

## Mechanic

Escape three levels in about 1–2 minutes by jumping from the highest
underground wheel above the ground line. Select Next level after each
escape. Level three completes the game. Later levels increase rotation
speed and add striped sections that bounce you away.

Hold Left/Right arrows or buttons to slide counterclockwise/clockwise.
Release to ride. Click Jump or press Space/Enter to launch with momentum.
There is no airborne steering. Lava ends the run. Pause suspends play,
while Restart resets all levels.

Choose the preview setting before starting. ON displays up to 1.5 seconds
of predicted trajectory, including bounces. OFF hides it. Other rules
remain constant.

## Dynamic

With preview ON, the playtester reported better timing and more confident
launch decisions. With OFF, trajectories had to be estimated from rotation
and position. Missed jumps sometimes led to recoverable falls onto lower
wheels. Practice between attempts may also have influenced this comparison.

## Aesthetic

The playtester described pressure from rising lava, heightened during
falls. Recoveries felt fortunate. ON provided reassurance, while OFF
increased insecurity and pressure. This contrast suggests that
unaided timing can make successful landings feel more earned, illustrating
Challenge and mastery.
