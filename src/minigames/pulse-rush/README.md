# Pulse Rush: feel the tempo climb

Motivator: Sensation. Author: HowrdWng.

## Mechanic

Play a 60-second round. When the circle says **PULSE!**, click or tap it, or press Space or Enter. Hits must land within 600 milliseconds. Counters show hits, misses, early presses, and the next gap.

The **Escalation** toggle applies at the start. On, every four hits shorten the gap by 0.1 seconds, from 1.2 toward a 0.4-second minimum; off, it stays at 1.2. Play both versions.

## Dynamic

The playtester said the game flow and rules were simple. With escalation on, four accurate hits shortened the gap from 1.2 to 1.1 seconds, so prompts arrived sooner. With it off, four hits left the gap at 1.2 seconds. Both runs had zero misses or early presses. Unexpectedly, the mechanic changed rhythm before performance.

## Aesthetic

The playtester described both versions as simple and easy to follow. Escalation on felt urgent because shrinking rests demanded continuous attention; off felt calmer and predictable. The strongest contrast was breathing room, not success or failure. Escalation therefore registered as rising stimulation, matching Sensation.

## Sources

The note defines Sensation as “game as sense-pleasure” (Hunicke et al. 2004, p. 2). Its escalation dynamic includes “invaders that speed up as you destroy them” (Schell 2019, p. 56). Successful hits similarly speed later pulses unless the switch is off.

Codex helped design and implement the game. I kept its suggestion to display the live gap, making the rule visible. No outside code, art, or sound is used.
