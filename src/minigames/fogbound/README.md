# Fogbound: leave a light

Motivator: Discovery. Author: JarrettChen217.

## Mechanic

Explore with WASD/arrows or direction buttons. E, Space, or Interact opens nearby chests and pulses outposts. Each outpost costs one supply and opens after three pulses, expanding sight. The first chest unlocks Shift Boost; release it to recharge while walking or standing. Open both outposts, claim the map, then collect marked stars/chests for “You got everything.” Click to start/resume; losing focus pauses.

Fog of war hides unknown terrain; torchlight reveals nearby tiles, and explored ground remains dim. Switching fog off reveals the camera viewport and restarts the round. Layout, arrows, movement and rewards remain identical.

## Dynamic

Another player restarted for ON then OFF, opening both outposts and collecting everything. OFF was 10 seconds faster. They followed arrows both ways; the designer observed earlier obstacle avoidance with fog off. The player needed equipment and Boost explanations.

## Aesthetic

Initially, the player reported little difference because both modes had arrows. After a leading explanation/question about discovery, they reported some exploration feeling and less interest without fog.
