# Battle Forge

Motivator: Progress, completion, and power (Facility rewards).
Author: yw4626.

## Mechanic

This is an arena game about progress and power. Move with WASD or touch controls; attacks fire automatically. The objective is to defeat all enemies. With “Combat unlocks abilities” enabled, defeating the first 6 drones gives the player Shockwave, which damages nearby enemies. Defeating the 1st boss gives Breach, which pierces enemies and breaks armor. With the toggle disabled, only basic shooting remains.

## Dynamic

With unlocks enabled, the tester approached the enemies more aggressively, seeking an opportunity to get near the boss and cause damage. With unlocks disabled, the tester avoided enemies more during the second stage, and the 1st boss kept summoning enemies. The abilities changed their combat behavior.

## Aesthetic

The playtester reported that the gameplay felt smooth with unlocks enabled. When the button was turned off, they felt frustrated and stopped trying when they reached the 1st boss. These responses supported the intention to make earned abilities produce a noticeable sense of growing power, while also showing how withholding those abilities can make combat discouraging.

## Sources

The course note describes facility rewards as “new or stronger abilities” (Salen and Zimmerman 2003, ch. 24, “Rewards and Schedules”). I asked Codex to design a game where players gradually become stronger. I liked and retained its arena concept, but rejected its suggestion of manually activated skills because they would overcomplicate gameplay, so I chose passive abilities instead. I also manually made some changes to the HP and damage to balance the gameplay. Codex assisted with design, implementation, and testing. Atlas template helpers are reused.
