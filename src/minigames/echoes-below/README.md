# Echoes Below

Motivator: Discovery.

## Mechanic

Explore a 30-by-20 sea, restore three beacons, and defeat the Leviathan. Move with WASD/arrows, aim with the mouse, and hold Space to fire forward. Q scans, R repairs, F toggles fog, and Escape pauses. On phones, use the left stick to move; hold and drag the right to aim and fire. Upgrades strengthen and evolve the submarine. Dodge enemy windups and use reefs as cover. With fog on, exploration and sonar reveal the sea. Disabling fog reveals the map, enemies, and items without changing AI, combat statistics, or loot: the switch changes access to information.

## Dynamic

The playtester described fog-on play as searching for beacons while exploring and fighting. With fog off, seeing all monsters and items made route planning easier. Play shifted toward combat because searching through unknown areas was no longer necessary. This contrast links the same combat system to two different patterns: searching for objectives versus planning around known encounters.

## Aesthetic

The playtester preferred fog on because it combined exploration, uncertainty, and challenge. Without fog, they found the game easier and more like straightforward combat, losing the sense of the unknown. Their preference suggests that discovering the sea was part of the enjoyment, alongside fighting its enemies.
