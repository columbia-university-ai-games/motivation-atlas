# Almost There

## Mechanic

Pack ten parcels. Press **Pack parcel**, wait six seconds, then press again
when ready. Extra presses during packing do nothing. Use touch, a mouse,
or Tab and Enter/Space. A run takes about one minute plus pauses between tasks.

**Show Progress** toggles a counter, progress bar, and milestone messages at
two, five, eight, and ten parcels. Both settings keep the same tasks, timing,
ready/packing cue, and final completion. Press **Play again** to compare settings.

## Dynamic

One participant tested both toggle settings. They reported that seeing the
progress bar motivated them to keep packing parcels. This playtest provides
one participant's account of how visible progress supported continuing the
repeated task. It does not establish a measured difference in persistence
between settings; no additional behavior was reported.

## Aesthetic

The participant reported feeling impatient without the progress bar. With
the bar visible, they reported motivation to keep packing. These are the
participant's reported feelings, rather than an assumed feeling of satisfaction
at completion. After playing, reflect: what did you feel, and did changing
the feedback change it?
