# Hidden Trail

Motivator: discovery. Author: raycarllei.

## Mechanic

Find three beacons marked B, then walk to E using buttons, arrow keys, or WASD.
Move one tile at a time; walls block movement. Hide unexplored tiles
reveals your surroundings as you move; turn it off to inspect the whole map.
Numbers count visits in both modes, distinguishing walked tiles from revealed
neighbors. The switch preserves progress. Restart returns
you to the start, clears visits and beacons, and keeps the same layout.

## Dynamic

Player A compared seeing the full map with revealing it while moving. With
only nearby tiles visible, they explored new areas as they moved. Both modes
share the board and goal. With the switch on, tiles appear around each
new position; with it off, the whole map is visible from the start. Replaying
the fixed layout gives the player opportunities to learn its routes.

## Aesthetic

Player A reported stronger exploration and satisfaction when entering new
areas with partial visibility. They also described each attempt as feeling
like a new game and felt the game was too random. Their responses suggest a
tradeoff between the pleasure of discovery and staying oriented.
