# Make 24

Motivator: Challenge. Author: the course.

## Mechanic

Press **Start** to draw six cards of numbers from 1 to 12. Click cards and operation buttons, or drag them into the formula row. Click a formula token to remove it. Use each number card at most once; use operations as often as you'd like. Parentheses and ordinary arithmetic precedence apply. Every division must produce a whole number. Press **= 24** to submit. A correct formula equals 24.

A formula cannot be submitted again in a commutative reordering, such as `2 + 3` and `3 + 2`; distributive rewrites are treated as different formulas. 

**Next hand** draws 6 new nummbers and clears that hand’s history. Turn scoring on or off at any time; changing it clears the history and score. Check **i** button for scoring rules.

## Dynamic

Six changing numbers create a concrete puzzle with a clear goal and immediate feedback. Choosing which numbers to combine, and whether to spend a division or parentheses, makes each hand a search for a workable plan. Scoring makes longer formulas and successive new solutions more rewarding; switching it off removes those incentives.

## Aesthetic

Challenge: the formula must satisfy a precise goal, while each new hand changes the available route to it. Notice whether finding a solution feels like overcoming an obstacle, and whether points change what you try.
