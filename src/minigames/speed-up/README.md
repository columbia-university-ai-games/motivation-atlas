# Speed Up: escalation

Motivator: Sensation. Author: SM5858.

## Mechanic

One cell of the 3×3 grid lights up. Tap it (or press its number key, 1 to 9) before it goes dark. Each hit scores a point and moves the light. A target that goes dark, or a tap on the wrong cell, costs one of your three lives. The round ends after 60 seconds or when your lives run out.

The toggle, applied when you press **Start**:

- **Escalation on:** every hit shortens how long the next target stays lit, from 1.2 seconds down to 0.4.
- **Escalation off:** every target stays lit for 1.2 seconds, the whole round.
