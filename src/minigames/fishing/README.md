# Gone Fishing: when do the bites come?

Motivator: Chance. Author: LuluLizzy8.

## Mechanic

You have 24 casts of bait. Press **Cast** and see whether a fish bites, or press **Pack up** to stop early. The row of marks records every cast.

The toggle, **Bites come at random**, changes when fish bite. On, each cast has a 1 in 4 chance. Off, every 4th cast bites. Both average 6 fish per round. Set it, then press **New round**.

## Dynamic

One playtester played with the toggle on, then off. With random bites, they kept casting, trying to out-fish another person and see how lucky they could get. With every 4th cast biting, they spotted the pattern, clicked through as fast as they could, and quit halfway. The same average catch kept them playing one way and made them quit the other. Unexpectedly, chance turned a solo game into a contest, so Competition appeared alongside Chance.

## Aesthetic

With random bites, they said it felt very random, and that was the fun: hoping for a lucky streak and a bigger catch than someone else's. With fixed bites, they said there was no point in playing, or in finishing, since they knew every 4th cast would bite. Removing the surprise removed the reason to keep casting.

## Sources

From the course note's Chance section:

- Variable-ratio reinforcement, the slot machine's schedule: "The repeat play of gamblers is strong evidence of the power of variable reinforcement" (Salen and Zimmerman 2003, ch. 24, "Rewards and Schedules").
- Variable rewards: a one-in-three chance of thirty points "stays rewarding for a much longer time" than ten points every time, though the average is the same (Schell 2019, p. 233).

The toggle swaps the variable schedule for a fixed one with the same average, so any change in persistence comes from the schedule alone.
