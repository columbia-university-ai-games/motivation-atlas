# Any Given Morning

Motivator: Meaning. Author: NICOH-YAY.

## Mechanic

Walk a small farm with WASD or arrows. E, Space or Enter talks, picks things up, gives them, or sits. There is no goal and no timer. With Mortality on, every life ends 16 to 24 seconds in, whatever you do, and you wake somewhere new. Five lives make a round. With Mortality off, you get one 100 second day. Touch devices get a pad and an A button. The ledger at the end lists what you did and asks what you felt.

## Dynamic

Wren, an AI playtester run through Codex, played five lives with Mortality on and one day with it off. On, Wren returned a hat in one life and gave a child a stone in another: 5 chosen deeds in 108 seconds. Off, Wren chose 3 deeds in 100 seconds and spent the rest testing a bench that did nothing, a bug now fixed.

## Aesthetic

With Mortality on, the short lives made Wren want to finish one more errand before disappearing. Wren was impatient by life four. With it off, Wren felt relaxed, then bored, with time but nothing new.
