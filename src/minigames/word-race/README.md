# Word Race

Motivator: Competition. Author: liangl5.

## Mechanic

Choose a bot difficulty and finish line before racing. Easy is 40 WPM, Normal
is 60, Hard is 80, and Expert is 100. The finish line can be 5, 10, or 20
points. Click **Begin race**, wait through the two-second countdown, then type
each displayed word; correct words score automatically. The bot uses the same
pre-generated, non-repeating words,
with timing adjusted for word length. Progress bars show each racer's distance
to the finish. Settings lock during a race and unlock after **New race**.

## Dynamic

Customizability lets the player choose a shorter or longer race and an easier
or harder opponent. In a 20-point playtest against Hard, the player won by only
one word. The long race gave the competition time to build, while the difficult
opponent kept the result uncertain. Easier bots and shorter races make the same
typing mechanic more approachable.

## Aesthetic

The player reported feeling accomplished after narrowly beating Hard. The
progress bars made each word visible movement toward victory, while the close
score created tension. The settings let the player choose a manageable
challenge or a more intense contest. The one-word margin made success feel
earned rather than automatic.

## Sources

The course note describes competition as adversarial play with “clear feedback
about who is winning,” and says that every player needs a winning condition
they can still believe in (Hunicke et al. 2004, p. 3). I used Codex while
building the game and kept its suggestions to show upcoming words and scores
as progress bars. I improved both suggestions so the race is easier to read
and plan. The code and visual design are original; no outside art, sound, or
code was borrowed.
